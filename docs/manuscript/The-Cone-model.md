# Quadrature

The essence of energy is its rotational nature. We envisage a phase arrow sweeping through a circle of energy. one wavelength - one rotation.
As they exchange energy, FoEs exchange dominance and direction.
Phase 1 - Primary energy dominates, in a +ve direction
Phase 2 - Derivative energy dominates, in  a +ve direction
Phase 3 - Primary energy dominates, in a -ve direction
Phase 4 - Derivative energy dominates in a -ve direction.
the cycle continues  and repeats at Phase 1.

A diagram of a sine and cosine wave together demonstrates this naturally. The rotation is inherent,  with the two waves being projects of the rotation onto real-world planes.
By convention, we consider Sine to be the Primary energy, and Cosine to be the derivative energy (maybe). Cosine is the derivative of Sine, (and vice versa), hence the 'derivative' terminology.
