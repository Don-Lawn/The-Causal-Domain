# **Dimensional Derivation (Pᵢ, Sᵢ, Qᵢ)**

## **1. Overview**

Each symmetry layer Li is defined by a triplet of axes:

- **Primary axis** Pi — the new dimension introduced
- **Secondary axis** Si — the derivative or dynamical expression
- **Q‑axis** Qi — the normal axis that becomes the locus of symmetry locks

This P–S–Q structure is the geometric grammar of the layered symmetry framework.

## **2. Primary Axis** Pi

The primary axis introduces a new degree of freedom.

Examples:

- P0= Tempo
- P1= Length
- P2= Force
- P3= Mass
- P4= Internal index
- P5= SUSY generator

## **3. Secondary Axis** Si

The secondary axis is the derivative or dynamical expression of the primary axis.

Examples:

- S0= Phase rate
- S1= Velocity
- S2= Field strength
- S3= Gravitational field
- S4= Interaction strength

## **4. Q‑Axis** Qi

The Q‑axis is orthogonal to the P–S plane. It is the axis along which boundaries act.

Examples:

- Q0= Phase normal
- Q2= Gauge normal
- Q3= Curvature normal
- Q4= Representation normal

## **5. Why the P–S–Q Structure Is Universal**

- Every symmetry lives in a plane (P–S).
- Every boundary acts along a normal (Q).
- Every lock is a Q‑lock.

This structure repeats identically across all layers, giving physics a consistent geometric foundation.
