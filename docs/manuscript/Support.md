# Support This Project

Rotational Relativity (RR) is an ongoing research effort exploring the geometry of the Q‑axis, Tempo, subjective time, and the unified φ/θ rotation structure. The work is developed independently, without institutional funding, and released openly so others can follow, critique, and build upon the ideas.

If you find the RR/Q geometry valuable — whether the conceptual framework, the mathematical structure, or the cosmological implications — you can support continued development.
