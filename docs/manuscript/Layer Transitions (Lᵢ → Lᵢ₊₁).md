# **Layer Transitions (Lᵢ → Lᵢ₊₁)**

## **1. Overview**

A **layer transition** occurs when the universe introduces a new **primary axis** Pi. This expands the dimensional structure of physical description and creates a new symmetry layer.

Layer transitions are the “big steps” in the architecture of physics.

## **2. The Sequence of Transitions**

### **L₀ → L₁: Tempo to Geometry**

- New axis: P1=L
- Space becomes a dimension
- Velocity S1 emerges
- Lorentz symmetry becomes possible

### **L₁ → L₂: Geometry to Electromagnetism**

- New axis: P2=F
- Force becomes independent of mass
- Charge appears
- U(1) gauge symmetry emerges

### **L₂ → L₃: Electromagnetism to Mass/Curvature**

- New axis: P3=m
- Curvature becomes meaningful
- Gravitational behaviour emerges
- Diffeomorphism invariance appears

### **L₃ → L₄: Curvature to Internal Symmetry**

- New axis: P4= internal index
- Color, flavor, spin become structural
- SU(3), SU(2), U(1) internal symmetries appear

### **L₄ → L₅: Internal to Meta‑Symmetry**

- New axis: P5=Q^
- Fermion–boson mixing becomes possible
- Unified gauge groups appear

## **3. What Layer Transitions Are Not**

- Not composition
- Not aggregation
- Not emergent behaviour in the loose sense
- Not a change in complexity

Layer transitions change **dimensionality**, not structure.

## **4. Why Layer Transitions Matter**

They reveal the deep architecture of physics:

- why space appears before charge
- why charge appears before mass
- why mass appears before internal symmetries
- why internal symmetries appear before SUSY

The sequence is not arbitrary — it is forced by dimensional logic.
