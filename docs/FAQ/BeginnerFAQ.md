# Beginner FAQ

## What is Relational Relativity (RR)?

Relational Relativity (RR) is a geometric framework that seeks to explain space, time, matter and energy as emergent properties of a deeper underlying reality known as the Causal Domain.

Rather than replacing existing physics, RR aims to provide an intuitive interpretation of the mechanisms from which familiar physical laws emerge.

------

## What is the Causal Domain?

The Causal Domain is the foundational wave-field substrate proposed by RR.

It is not the spacetime we directly observe. Instead, observable reality is viewed as a projection or manifestation of deeper processes occurring within the Causal Domain.

See also: Causal Domain, Phenomenal Domain.

------

## What is the Phenomenal Domain?

The Phenomenal Domain is the reality we experience directly: space, time, matter, energy and physical events.

In RR, the Phenomenal Domain emerges from structures and processes within the deeper Causal Domain.

See also: Causal Domain.

------

## What is Tempo?

Tempo describes the relationship between progression in the Causal Domain and the passage of time in the Phenomenal Domain.

RR proposes that many relativistic effects can be understood as variations in Tempo.

See also: Tempo, Time, Relational Special Relativity.

------

## What is the Q-Dimension?

The Q-Dimension is an additional degree of freedom used within RR to describe depth, curvature and geometric structure beyond ordinary spacetime.

It plays an important role in discussions of gravity, matter and the geometry of physical reality.

See also: Q-Dimension, Q-Depth.

------

## What is Energy-Mass Duality?

RR treats energy and mass as different manifestations of the same underlying structures.

Mass may be understood as energy in a highly constrained or low-tempo configuration, while energy may be viewed as a more dynamic expression of the same underlying reality.

See also: Energy-Mass Duality.

------

## What is the Ribbon Photon?

The Ribbon Photon is a geometric model of the photon used within RR.

Rather than treating a photon as a simple point or wave, RR models it as a structured rotational object whose geometry helps explain electromagnetic phenomena and particle formation.

See also: Ribbon Photon.

------

## What is Gravity in RR?

RR interprets gravity geometrically.

Rather than being a force acting through empty space, gravity arises from geometric relationships within the underlying structure of reality, including the Q-Dimension.

See also: Gravity, Q-Dimension, Relational General Relativity.

------

## How does RR differ from conventional physics?

RR generally seeks to preserve the successful mathematics and predictions of established physics.

Its primary goal is to provide a deeper geometric interpretation of why physical laws take the forms that they do.

------

## Does RR change our understanding of black holes?

Yes. One notable difference concerns the interpretation of black holes.

RR proposes that the event horizon itself acts as the effective singularity. Objects approaching the horizon become increasingly time-locked through extreme time dilation.

In simple terms:

**Nothing falls into a black hole. Things fall onto it.**

See also: Black Holes, Tempo, Relational General Relativity.

------

## How does RR reinterpret redshift?

RR explores the possibility that at least some observed redshift effects arise from projection geometry and Tempo relationships, rather than being solely the result of spatial expansion.

See also: Redshift, Cosmology, Tempo.

------

## Is RR compatible with established science?

RR aims to remain consistent with established observations, experiments and mathematical descriptions of nature.

Its purpose is not to discard modern physics, but to provide a unified geometric framework from which familiar physics may emerge naturally.
